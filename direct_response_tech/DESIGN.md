## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an elite direct-response sales optimization platform powered by artificial intelligence. The personality is authoritative, engineered for measurable revenue generation, ultra-modern, and mathematically precise. It projects the surgical speed of modern software alongside the high-stakes psychological acuity of top-tier growth marketing.

The aesthetic fuses modern high-performance SaaS conventions with a dual-surface framework: dense, immersive obsidian-navy command rails (#0B0F19 / #111827) paired with an ultra-crisp, distraction-free neutral canvas (#F8FAFC and #FFFFFF). Key visual characteristics include micro-bordered card tiers (1px subtle border transitions), high-clarity telemetry dashboards, precision pill badges for live conversion tracking, and crisp focus rings. Visual noise is stripped away to highlight funnel performance metrics, drop-off heatmaps, and modular block configurations.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate hierarchy between platform architecture, active canvas states, and real-time revenue telemetry:

- **Command Rail & Structural Obsidian:** The sidebar, top header, and technical dock utilize deep structural dark values (`#0B0F19` as the foundation, `#111827` for elevated layers, with `#1E293B` micro-lines) to ground the interface in an executive engineering atmosphere.
- **Canvas & Surface Neutral:** The active builder viewport uses `#F8FAFC` for page canvas backgrounds, with elevated cards resting on pure `#FFFFFF` bound by `#E2E8F0` micro-borders.
- **Primary Electric Indigo (`#4F46E5`):** Reserved for primary interactive controls, funnel flow connections, active builder nodes, and AI prompt triggers.
- **Secondary Royal Blue (`#2563EB`):** Applied to active selection anchors, secondary interactive paths, structural links, and configuration controls.
- **Conversion Emerald (`#10B981`):** A high-converting revenue indicator applied exclusively to positive conversion deltas, live publish states, checkout rate gains, and high-priority primary checkout CTA previews.
- **Direct-Response Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Used strictly for conversion rate friction warnings, urgency badge previews, split-test variants, and alert telemetry.

## Typography

The type scale pairs the structured geometric punch of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, builder module titles, and key conversion statistics with the hyper-legible neutral cadence of **Inter** for canvas copy, data readouts, and configuration panels.

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Tight negative letter-spacing creates compact, confident lockups suitable for high-converting marketing copy and executive analytics.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`):** Renders clean data density in compact telemetry panels, input forms, and step-by-step funnel sequences.
- **Metrics & Numbers (`metric-display`):** Numerical metrics leverage tabular lining figures in `Plus Jakarta Sans` to guarantee strict vertical alignment across revenue telemetry rows and real-time split-test calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses a split-environment layout model:
1. **Persistent Structural Rails:** A fixed 260px left navigation rail and a dynamic 340px right-hand module inspector docking framework, both anchored with internal zero-gutter padding.
2. **Central Workspace Canvas:** An adjustable, centered viewport simulation environment (desktop: 1200px, tablet: 768px, mobile: 375px) rendered over an infinite fluid canvas grid.
3. **Telemetry & Funnel Grids:** 12-column responsive fluid grid across analytics and funnel step overviews, shifting to 4 columns on mobile viewports (<640px) and 8 columns on tablet viewports (640px–1024px).

Components follow an absolute 4px-baseline unit rhythm, where layout groupings default to `space-md` (16px) for interior card groups and `space-lg` (24px) for distinct functional sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses fine-gauge micro-borders paired with low-saturation, ambient light dissipation instead of heavy, muddy dropshadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Surface):** Neutral foundation (`#F8FAFC`). Purely flat with no depth.
- **Level 1 (Card & Inspector Panes):** `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, paired with an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Drag Nodes & Interactive Modals):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border, lifted by: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (AI Generation Engine & Critical Overlays):** Deep navy translucent backdrops (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.60)` with 8px backdrop blur) supporting elevated cards styled with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Selected Workspace Node Highlight:** Zero elevation shift, replacing neutral borders with a crisp 1.5px solid `#4F46E5` edge and a 3px soft outer ring (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The design uses a clean, engineered corner language (Level 2):
- **Base Surfaces & Inputs:** 0.5rem (8px) corner radius for input fields, workspace nodes, module panels, and metric summary tiles.
- **Cards & Modular Blocks:** 0.75rem (12px) to 1rem (16px) for main structural cards, funnel canvas frames, and popup modal wrappers.
- **Pills & Status Tags:** Fully rounded (9999px) for telemetry indicators, live A/B experiment badges, live conversion counters, and interactive step tabs.
- **Action Buttons:** Balanced 0.5rem (8px) radius to maintain an intentional, crisp corporate edge without feeling overly circular or juvenile.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct Response / Conversion):** `#4F46E5` solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold `label-md` typography, 0.5rem corner radius, padded 0.625rem vertical by 1.25rem horizontal. Subtle hover lift with background transition to `#4338CA`.
- **High-Converting Revenue CTAs:** High-impact `#10B981` background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle 1px border (`#059669`), paired with a slight inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#1E293B`, shifting to `#F1F5F9` background on hover.
- **Command Rail Actions:** Semi-transparent dark surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, text in `#F8FAFC`.

### Form Fields & Inspector Inputs
- Text inputs, metric steppers, and formula inputs use a 40px height standard, `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, and a 0.5rem radius.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#4F46E5` accompanied by a 3px outer glow ring in `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`. Labels use `label-md` set in `#475569`.

### Badges & Status Pills
- Compact, fully rounded height profile (22px to 26px).
- **Live / Uplift:** Pale emerald fill (`#ECFDF5`), deep emerald text (`#065F46`), with a 1px border (`#A7F3D0`) and an integrated 6px solid `#10B981` pulse dot.
- **Split Test Variant:** Pale indigo fill (`#EEF2FF`), electric indigo text (`#3730A3`), 1px border (`#C7D2FE`).
- **Friction / Warning:** Light amber fill (`#FFFBEB`), deep amber text (`#92400E`), 1px border (`#FDE68A`).

### Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- Surface set to pure `#FFFFFF`, edged with a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, padded at `1.25rem`. 
- Header rows separate micro-copy titles (`label-sm` uppercase in `#64748B`) from absolute conversion figures (`metric-display` in `#0F172A`), terminating in trend micro-pills at the right edge.

### Interactive Modular Builder Tabs
- Tab groups use an encased segmented design: `#F1F5F9` container with a 0.5rem radius, inset with 4px padding. 
- Active tab uses a `#FFFFFF` elevated pill with a 1px border in `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` and `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` shadow, while inactive tabs use `#64748B` typography that transitions to `#0F172A` on hover.

### Funnel Step Nodes
- Modular canvas blocks displaying funnel flow paths. Built on a 280px-wide card with an icon anchor, conversion percentage badge, drop-off rate denominator, and a right-facing connecting anchor node colored in `#4F46E5`.
