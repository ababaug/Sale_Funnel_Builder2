## Questions creators ask before switching.

### How do installments work for my buyers?

They choose a plan at checkout and pay in weekly or monthly parts. Access unlocks when the final part clears, and you receive the full price.

### Where does my money go?

Sales land in your Atisah balance. Withdraw to your bank account or mobile money wallet whenever you’re ready.

### Can I sell to buyers outside Nigeria?

Yes. Price in 12 currencies, including cedis, shillings, rand, dollars, pounds and euros. Dollar and pound sales are 7%, processing included.
